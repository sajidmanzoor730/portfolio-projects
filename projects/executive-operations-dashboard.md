# Executive Operations Dashboard

## 🎯 Business Problem
Operations teams need visibility into meetings, priorities, owners, deadlines, and outstanding actions so that important work does not get lost.

## 📊 What It Shows
- Meeting and calendar tracking
- Priority management
- Action-item ownership
- Deadline monitoring
- Follow-up status
- Executive-style operational reporting

## 🧠 Reporting Workflow
**Operational records → status and priority tracking → summary reporting → follow-up visibility**

## 🛠️ Tools
Excel
